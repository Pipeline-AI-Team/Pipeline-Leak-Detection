╔═════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╗
║                     IMPORTANT NOTES & SAFETY PRECAUTIONS                    ║
╠═════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╣
║                                                                             ║
║  ⚠️  POWER SUPPLY:                                                          ║
║    • Use only 3.3V for BMP280 and DHT22 (NOT 5V - will damage them)       ║
║    • MQ-4/MQ-5 sensor can use 5V, but we'll use 3.3V for simplicity       ║
║    • Good external 3.3V power supply recommended for stable readings       ║
║                                                                             ║
║  ⚠️  I2C PULL-UP RESISTORS:                                                 ║
║    • 4.7K pull-up resistors for SDA and SCL lines                         ║
║    • Most breakout modules have these built-in                            ║
║    • Don't add extra resistors if already on module                       ║
║                                                                             ║
║  ⚠️  GPIO VOLTAGE LEVELS:                                                   ║
║    • ESP32 GPIO pins accept 3.3V maximum                                  ║
║    • Don't connect 5V sensors directly to GPIO pins                       ║
║    • Use voltage divider (10K:10K) if sensor outputs 5V                   ║
║                                                                             ║
║  ⚠️  SENSOR PLACEMENT:                                                      ║
║    • MQ-4 sensor needs 20-30s warm-up time after power on                 ║
║    • Keep sensors away from vibrations and magnetic fields                ║
║    • Ensure good ventilation around gas sensor                            ║
║    • BMP280 should be at pipeline measurement point                       ║
║    • DHT22 works best at 20-80% humidity, 0-50°C                          ║
║                                                                             ║
║  ⚠️  WIRING QUALITY:                                                        ║
║    • Use good quality jumper wires (not thin/low gauge)                   ║
║    • Keep wires short where possible (<1 meter)                           ║
║    • Use breadboard correctly to avoid loose connections                  ║
║    • Double-check all connections before power on                        ║
║                                                                             ║
║  ✅ TESTING BEFORE DEPLOYMENT:                                             ║
║    • Test each sensor individually first                                  ║
║    • Verify readings in Serial Monitor (baud 115200)                      ║
║    • Check I2C address with I2C scanner sketch                            ║
║    • Ensure WiFi connection works                                         ║
║    • Log data to SD card as backup                                        ║
║                                                                             ║
